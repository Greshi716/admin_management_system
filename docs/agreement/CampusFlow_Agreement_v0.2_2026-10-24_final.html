Important principles

  • Nothing financial is ever deleted. A wrong receipt is cancelled with a reason; the number stays in the register.
  • Leaving students and staff are not erased. They move to the LC / Leaving register and disappear from active lists, but history is kept.
  • A finalised payroll month is locked. Later rule changes apply only from their effective month.
  • All amounts are exact to the paisa; rounding follows rules approved by the school.

Receipt rules

  • Receipt number is generated automatically, never repeated, never skipped. To confirm continue from current book or restart each year?
  • One receipt can contain several fee heads (as in the book).
  • Payment mode: Cash or Cheque (cheque no. and date). To confirm UPI / bank transfer?

12

Assumptions & Scope

Assumptions

  • One school, one campus.
  • The system runs on one computer in the school; other office computers open it in a browser over the school network.
  • Internet is not required for daily use (only for importing Google Form entries).
  • Current students, staff, this year's payments and the opening balance will be imported from Excel.
  • For 1–2 months after go-live, Excel and the system will be used side by side.
  • Screens in English. Gujarati for notices / messages to be confirmed.

Not included in version 1

  • Online fee payment by parents (payment gateway).
  • Automatic sending of SMS / WhatsApp — the system prepares the message.
  • Attendance, exams & marks, timetable, library, transport.
  • Parent or student login / mobile app.
  • Government portal uploads (EPF ECR, income tax returns).
  • Cloud hosting.
13

Installation, Updates & Data Safety

🖥️

Installation

We install the application and database on the school computer. A desktop shortcut opens CampusFlow.

⬆️

Updates

One update file. It backs up first, then updates. If something fails, the previous version is restored.

💾

Backups

Automatic daily backup. Last 7 days, 4 weeks and 12 months kept. Dashboard shows the last backup time.

🔐

Privacy

Data stays inside the school. Bank, EPF, Aadhaar and salary details are visible only to authorised roles.

School's responsibilities

  • Provide one computer that stays on during office hours (recommended: 8 GB RAM, 100 GB free). Actual machine: ____________
  • Copy the backup folder to a USB drive or Google Drive once a week (the system will remind you).
  • Use a UPS for the server computer.
  • Keep admin passwords private; create a separate login for each person.
